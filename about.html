Our Story

Built Around Automotive Care

WashOn was created to bring professional-grade car wash, detailing, servicing and lubricant expertise together in one dependable destination — so vehicle owners no longer have to visit separate shops for separate needs. Founded by a team of automotive enthusiasts who saw a need for a cleaner, simpler and more reliable car experience, WashOn is built on a passion for vehicles, a commitment to quality, and a belief that every driver deserves the best care.

Mission & Vision
